Hi, I am Martin

Hi, I'm Martin Šrámek, a graphic designer, video editor, and IT student who believes that technology and creativity are inextricably linked. I base my work not only on visual aesthetics but also on technical precision and understanding the client's needs. The combination of analytical thinking from IT studies and an eye for detail, which I gained through my apprenticeship as a reproduction graphic designer, allows me to create content that not only looks good but primarily works – whether it's a dynamic video for social media, clean product photography, or graphic design. I don't just draw from theory; I actively edit videos for the Tisicikoruna project, collaborate with marketing agencies, and shoot for local businesses, giving me direct insight into what actually sells on the market today. Thanks to my IT studies, I am not afraid of challenges like 3D modeling, web development, or working with hardware, allowing me to offer a comprehensive approach from the initial design to the final realization under one roof. When I'm not working in an editing bay or graphic editor, you can find me at the gym, training parkour, or working with 3D printing and electronics. Let's create something meaningful together—whether you need to photograph products, edit a viral video, or build a turnkey website, I'd be happy to help take your project to the next level.
