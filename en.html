About Me

Behind the lens & keyboard

Hi, I'm Martin Šrámek, a creative freelancer, video editor, and developer. I build custom websites, stich engaging videos, and design hardware. I believe technology and design are tightly bound together.

Combining analytical IT thinking and graphical detail allows me to offer all-in-one solutions under one roof – from dynamic editing and branding assets to IoT hardware and custom plošných spojů (PCB). I edit video for the Tisicikoruna project, build Klipni.eu, and work with marketing firms.
